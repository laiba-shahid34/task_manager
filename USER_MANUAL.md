# User Manual - Dreamy Task Manager

## 1. Install the app
1. Download `release/task_manager.apk` from the GitHub repository to your phone.
2. Open the file and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
3. Open **Task Manager** and tap **Get Started** on the welcome screen.
4. Tap **Allow** when the app asks for notification permission.

## 2. Home screen
- The top card shows how many of today's tasks are done.
- The chips (**All, Today, Pending, Completed, Important, Repeating, Overdue**) filter the list.
- Use the search box to find a task by text.
- The bottom bar has **Home, Calendar, Stats and Settings**.
- The music button on the top turns the background music on or off.

## 3. Add a task
1. Tap the **+** button.
2. Enter a **title** (required) and an optional **description**.
3. Choose a **category** and a **priority** (Low, Medium, High).
4. Pick the **due date and time**.
5. Keep **Add reminder** on to get a notification.
6. Choose **Repeat** if needed (see section 6).
7. Tap **Save**.

## 4. Edit, complete and delete
- **Open** a task to see its details, subtasks and progress. Use the edit button to change it.
- **Complete**: tap the circle next to the task, or swipe it to mark it as done. It appears in the **Completed** view.
- **Delete**: swipe the task. Tap **Undo** in the message at the bottom if you deleted it by mistake.
- **Star**: mark a task as important to find it in the **Important** filter.

## 5. Subtasks and progress
1. Open a task.
2. Add subtasks under **Subtasks**.
3. Tick each subtask when it is done. The progress bar and percentage update automatically.
4. Completing the main task completes all its subtasks.

## 6. Repeating tasks
- In the add/edit screen choose **Every day** or **Selected days** and tap the weekdays you want.
- When you complete a repeating task, it goes to Completed and the task jumps to its next date with the subtasks reset.
- All repeating tasks are listed under the **Repeating** chip.

## 7. Reminders
- A notification is shown at the due time, also when the app is closed.
- If the task is still not completed, you get follow-up reminders after **10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day and 1 week**. Completing the task cancels them.
- Choose the sound in **Settings > Reminder sound**:
  - Dreamy chime, Crystal bell, Soft pluck
  - Phone default
  - Silent
  - **Loud alarm**: keeps ringing until you open or dismiss the notification (uses the alarm volume of the phone)
- Use **Test now** and **Test in 10 seconds** to check your reminders.

## 8. Calendar
Open the **Calendar** tab, tap any day to see its tasks and press **+** to add a task on that day.

## 9. Stats
The **Stats** tab shows total, completed, pending and overdue tasks, your streak of days with completed tasks and a chart of recently completed tasks.

## 10. Settings
- **Theme**: Light, Dark or System.
- **Your name**: shown on the home screen.
- **Music**: on/off and volume.
- **Reminder sound** and notification tests.
- **Export as CSV**, **Export as PDF** or **Send by email** (choose Gmail in the share sheet).
- **Clear completed tasks** and **Delete everything**.

## 11. Troubleshooting
| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| No notification | Settings > Apps > Task Manager > Notifications must be ON. Tap **Allow** in the app Settings. |
| Notification does not come when the app is closed | Set Battery to **Unrestricted** and do not use Force stop. |
| No music sound | Increase the phone's **media volume** and the music slider in Settings. |
| Alarm is quiet | Increase the phone's **alarm volume**. |
